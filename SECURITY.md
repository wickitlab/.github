# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security problems privately. In a public repository, open the **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**: the report stays between you and WickIT Lab until a fix is out. You can also email support@wickitlab.com.

Please do not open a public issue for a security problem.

## What to expect

Reports are triaged weekly. You get an acknowledgement within a few business days, and a coordinated disclosure timeline agreed with you once the report is triaged. Fixes ship in a new release, and the advisory is published after users have had a reasonable window to update.

## Scope

This policy covers every repository in the wickitlab organization that has no security policy of its own. A repository's own policy takes precedence.
